# Lunch phase controls and visibility

## Scenarios

- After someone finishes meal collection, an admin or the selection creator in the same office can reopen it for 5, 10, or 15 minutes while no one has claimed ordering. Existing meals and the restaurant carry forward. Everyone sees the updated phase and deadline live.
- A claim blocks reopening. Concurrent claim and reopen requests cannot both succeed. Delivery and completed selections cannot be reopened.
- Participants can see the current step and who closed or reopened meal selection, claimed ordering, or confirmed the restaurant order. Older selections display only available attribution.
